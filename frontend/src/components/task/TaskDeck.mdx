# TaskDeck

A swipeable card stack that renders tasks as a deck — the top card fully visible, with the
next `stackSize` cards scaled and fanned behind it (Deck Standard 1 style). Drag the top card
past the swipe threshold to advance the deck; it can also **auto-rotate** on a timer and
**loop** forever.

`TaskDeck` is the **presentational** component — it takes tasks as props and does no fetching.
For a self-fetching version, see `TaskDeckWrapper`.

## Default

A deck of six tasks, ordered by urgency (unset urgency sorts last).

## AutoRotating

The deck advances automatically on `autoRotateMs` (2s in this story). The timer pauses during
a drag and resets after a manual skip.

## NoLoop

With `loop` disabled, the deck stops at the last card instead of wrapping back to the first.
